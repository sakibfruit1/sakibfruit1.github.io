🌿 VITAMINS & MINERALS

Essential Nutrients in Apples

Apples contain several vitamins, minerals and plant compounds that contribute to everyday health.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

⚡

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🦴

Vitamin K

Contributes to normal blood clotting and healthy bones.

🌿

Vitamin B6

Helps support normal energy metabolism and nervous system function.

🛡️

Polyphenols

Natural plant compounds with antioxidant properties, found especially in the skin.

✨

Antioxidants

Help protect cells from oxidative damage as part of a balanced diet.
