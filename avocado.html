Vitamins & Minerals in Avocado

Avocado contains several vitamins, minerals and beneficial plant compounds that contribute to everyday nutrition.

🥑

Vitamin K

Helps support normal blood clotting and bone health.

🌿

Vitamin E

Acts as an antioxidant that helps protect body cells.

🍃

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and collagen formation.

💚

Potassium

Helps maintain normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🌱

Folate

Supports normal cell growth and DNA formation.

🥗

Vitamin B6

Helps the body use energy and supports normal nerve function.

WHY AVOCADO MATTERS

Health Benefits of Avocado

Avocado provides healthy fats, fibre and essential nutrients that can contribute to a balanced lifestyle.

❤️

Supports Heart Health

Its monounsaturated fats can help support healthy cholesterol levels when used in place of saturated fats.

🌿

Supports Digestion

Its dietary fibre helps support regular digestion and healthy bowel function.

💪

Provides Healthy Fats

Avocado contains mostly unsaturated fats that provide energy and support normal body functions.

🧠

Supports Brain Function

Its healthy fats and nutrients contribute to normal brain and nervous system function.

✨

Provides Antioxidants

Vitamins C and E help protect body cells from oxidative damage.

🥗

Supports Healthy Eating

Its fibre and healthy fats can help make meals satisfying and nutritious.

MAKE IT PART OF YOUR DAY

How to Enjoy Avocado

Avocado is versatile and easy to include in everyday meals. Here are some simple ways to enjoy it.

🥑

Eat It Fresh

Cut a ripe avocado in half, remove the seed and enjoy its creamy flesh.

🥪

Avocado Toast

Mash avocado and spread it over whole-grain bread for a simple meal.

🥗

Fresh Salads

Add avocado slices to vegetable salads for a creamy texture.

🥤

Avocado Smoothies

Blend avocado with milk or yoghurt and fruit for a creamy smoothie.
