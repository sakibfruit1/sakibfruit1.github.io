ESSENTIAL NUTRIENTS

Vitamins & Minerals in Guava

Guava contains several important vitamins, minerals and plant compounds that contribute to everyday nutrition.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function, collagen formation and iron absorption.

🌿

Vitamin A

Helps support normal vision and immune function.

💚

Potassium

Helps maintain normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🌱

Folate

Supports normal cell growth and DNA formation.

🍃

Vitamin B6

Helps the body use energy and supports normal nerve function.

✨

Antioxidants

Guava contains plant compounds that help protect cells from oxidative damage.

WHY GUAVA MATTERS

Health Benefits of Guava

Guava provides vitamin C, fibre and beneficial plant compounds that can contribute to a healthy lifestyle.

🛡️

Supports Immune Function

Its high vitamin C content helps support normal immune system function.

🌿

Supports Digestion

Its dietary fibre helps support regular bowel movements and healthy digestion.

✨

Supports Skin Health

Vitamin C contributes to collagen formation, which helps maintain healthy skin.

❤️

Supports Heart Health

Its fibre and potassium contribute to a balanced eating pattern that supports cardiovascular health.

👀

Supports Healthy Vision

Guava contains carotenoids that contribute to normal eye health.

🍃

Provides Antioxidants

Its vitamin C and other plant compounds help protect cells from oxidative damage.

🍈 ENJOY GUAVA

Delicious Ways to Enjoy Guava

Guava is a versatile fruit that can be enjoyed in different refreshing and delicious ways.

🍈

Eat It Fresh

Wash a ripe guava and enjoy it fresh as a naturally sweet and nutritious snack.

🥤

Guava Smoothies

Blend ripe guava with milk or yoghurt for a refreshing smoothie.

🥗

Fruit Salads

Cut guava into small pieces and combine it with your favourite fruits.

🧃

Fresh Guava Juice

Blend guava with water and strain if desired for a refreshing drink.

🌿 UNDERSTAND YOUR NUTRITION

Guava and Essential Nutrients

Guava contains several nutrients that help your body function properly. Here are a few important examples.

🍊

Vitamin C

Helps support normal immune function, collagen production and wound healing. A lack of vitamin C can cause scurvy.

🌱

Folate

Helps the body produce DNA and healthy red blood cells. Folate deficiency can contribute to certain types of anaemia.

💚

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function, nerve signals and fluid balance. Severe deficiency can affect muscle function.

Important Nutrition Note

Guava can contribute to a balanced diet, but eating guava alone cannot prevent or treat nutrient deficiencies. A varied diet and appropriate medical advice are important.
