Health Benefits of Guava

Guava provides vitamin C, fibre and beneficial plant compounds that can contribute to a healthy lifestyle.

🛡️

Supports Immune Function

Its high vitamin C content helps support normal immune system function.

🌿

Supports Digestion

Its dietary fibre helps support regular bowel movements and healthy digestion.

✨

Supports Skin Health

Vitamin C contributes to collagen formation, which helps maintain healthy skin.

❤️

Supports Heart Health

Its fibre and potassium contribute to a balanced eating pattern that supports cardiovascular health.

👀

Supports Healthy Vision

Guava contains carotenoids that contribute to normal eye health.

🍃

Provides Antioxidants

Its vitamin C and other plant compounds help protect cells from oxidative damage.
