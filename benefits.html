THE BENEFITS OF FRUITS

Six Reasons to Enjoy More Fruits.

Discover some of the important ways fruits contribute to a healthy and balanced diet.

🛡️

Provides Essential Nutrients

Fruits contain vitamins and minerals that support normal body functions and help maintain good health.

🌾

Supports Digestion

Many fruits contain dietary fibre, which helps support normal digestion and regular bowel movements.

💧

Helps with Hydration

Fruits such as watermelon and oranges contain plenty of water, contributing to your daily fluid intake.

❤️

Supports Heart Health

Fruits provide fibre, potassium and other nutrients that can contribute to a heart-healthy eating pattern.

⚡

Provides Natural Energy

Fruits contain natural carbohydrates that provide energy for everyday activities.

🌈

Provides Antioxidants

Many fruits contain antioxidants and other plant compounds that help protect cells from oxidative damage.

WHAT DIFFERENT FRUITS OFFER

Different Fruits, Different Goodness.

Every fruit has its own nutritional qualities. Here are a few examples of nutrients found in popular fruits.

🍊

Oranges

A good source of vitamin C, which supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

🍌

Bananas

Provide potassium, vitamin B6 and carbohydrates that contribute to everyday energy needs.

🍉

Watermelon

Contains a high amount of water and provides vitamin C and other beneficial plant compounds.

🥑

Avocados

Provide healthy monounsaturated fats, fibre and potassium.

🍍

Pineapples

Contain vitamin C and manganese, along with the natural enzyme bromelain.

🍎

Apples

Provide dietary fibre and natural plant compounds that contribute to a balanced diet.
