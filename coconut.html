🥥 KNOW WHAT YOU EAT

Coconut Nutrition Facts

Coconut flesh provides energy, dietary fibre and several important nutrients. Here is an approximate nutritional breakdown.

Raw Coconut Flesh

Per 100g
354 Calories (kcal)
15.2g Carbohydrates
9g Dietary Fibre
3.3g Protein
33.5g Total Fat
6.2g Natural Sugars

Note: Values are approximate and may vary depending on coconut variety and maturity. Coconut flesh is relatively high in saturated fat.

🌿 NUTRIENTS THAT MATTER

Vitamins & Minerals in Coconut

Coconut contains several minerals and small amounts of vitamins that contribute to normal body functions.

⚡

Manganese

Helps support normal energy metabolism and healthy bone formation.

🩸

Copper

Supports iron metabolism, connective tissue formation and normal nerve function.

💪

Iron

Helps the body produce haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood.

🛡️

Selenium

Supports normal thyroid function and protects cells from oxidative damage.

🦴

Phosphorus

Helps maintain healthy bones and teeth and supports energy production.

💚

Potassium

Helps maintain fluid balance and supports normal muscle and nerve function.

🥥 WHY COCONUT MATTERS

Health Benefits of Coconut

Coconut provides nutrients that contribute to different functions in the body. Discover some ways it can fit into a balanced diet.

🌿

Provides Dietary Fibre

Coconut flesh contains fibre that supports normal digestion and helps maintain bowel health.

⚡

Provides Energy

Its natural fat content provides a concentrated source of energy for the body.

🦴

Supports Bone Health

Coconut contains minerals such as manganese and phosphorus that contribute to bone health.

🛡️

Provides Antioxidant Nutrients

Coconut contains compounds and minerals involved in the body's antioxidant systems.

💚

Provides Essential Minerals

It contributes minerals such as copper, manganese and potassium to the diet.

🥥

Versatile Food Choice

Coconut can be enjoyed in different meals and recipes as part of a varied diet.

🥥 ENJOY COCONUT

Delicious Ways to Enjoy Coconut

Coconut is a versatile tropical fruit that can be enjoyed in different refreshing and delicious ways.

🥥

Fresh Coconut Flesh

Enjoy fresh coconut flesh as a naturally rich and satisfying snack.

🥤

Coconut Water

Drink fresh coconut water as a refreshing beverage, especially on warm days.

🥣

Coconut Smoothies

Blend coconut flesh with fruits and milk to prepare a creamy tropical smoothie.

🍚

Coconut in Meals

Add coconut milk or grated coconut to suitable dishes for a tropical flavour.

🌿 NUTRIENT AWARENESS

Nutrients Coconut Contributes

Coconut provides several important nutrients. Including it in a balanced diet can help contribute to your daily nutrient intake.

⚙️

Manganese

Helps support normal bone formation, metabolism and antioxidant activity.

🩸

Iron

Helps the body produce haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood.

🛡️

Copper

Contributes to iron metabolism, connective tissue and normal nerve function.

Important: Coconut can contribute to nutrient intake, but eating coconut alone cannot prevent or treat nutrient deficiencies. A varied, balanced diet is important.
