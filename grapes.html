🌿 VITAMINS & MINERALS

Essential Nutrients in Grapes

Grapes contain several vitamins, minerals and natural plant compounds that contribute to everyday health.

🦴

Vitamin K

Contributes to normal blood clotting and helps maintain healthy bones.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

⚡

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🌿

Vitamin B6

Helps support energy metabolism and normal nervous system function.

🛡️

Polyphenols

Natural plant compounds with antioxidant properties, especially in darker grapes.

✨

Antioxidants

Help protect cells from oxidative damage as part of a balanced diet.

❤️ HEALTH BENEFITS

How Grapes Support Your Health

Grapes provide useful nutrients and natural plant compounds that can contribute to a balanced and healthy lifestyle.

🛡️

Provides Antioxidants

Grapes contain polyphenols and other antioxidants that help protect cells from oxidative damage.

❤️

Supports Heart Health

Their plant compounds and potassium contribute to a heart-healthy eating pattern.

🌿

Supports Digestion

Grapes contain water and dietary fibre that contribute to normal digestion.

💧

Supports Hydration

Their high water content helps contribute to your body's daily fluid intake.

🦴

Supports Bone Health

Grapes provide vitamin K, which contributes to the maintenance of normal bones.

⚡

Provides Natural Energy

Their natural carbohydrates provide energy for everyday activities.

🍇 ENJOY EVERY BITE

Delicious Ways to Enjoy Grapes

Grapes are versatile, refreshing and easy to include in everyday meals and snacks.

🍇

Eat Them Fresh

Wash fresh grapes and enjoy them as a naturally sweet everyday snack.

🥗

Fruit Salads

Combine grapes with other fresh fruits for a colourful and refreshing salad.

🥤

Grape Smoothies

Blend grapes with yoghurt or other fruits to make a refreshing smoothie.

❄️

Enjoy Them Chilled

Refrigerate grapes before eating for a cool and refreshing snack.
