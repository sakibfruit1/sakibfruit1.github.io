Orange Nutrition Facts

Oranges are refreshing citrus fruits that provide vitamin C, dietary fibre and other essential nutrients.

Nutrition per 100g

Raw orange
47 Calories (kcal)
11.8g Carbohydrates
2.4g Dietary Fibre
0.9g Protein
0.1g Total Fat
9.4g Natural Sugars

Values are approximate and may vary depending on orange variety and ripeness.

🌱 ESSENTIAL NUTRIENTS

Vitamins & Minerals in Orange

Oranges contain important vitamins and minerals that help the body perform everyday functions.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function, collagen production and helps the body absorb iron from plant foods.

🌿

Folate

Helps the body make DNA and supports normal cell growth and development.

💚

Potassium

Supports normal muscle function, nerve signals and healthy blood pressure.

⚡

Vitamin B1

Helps the body convert food into energy and supports normal nerve function.

🦴

Calcium

Contributes to normal bone and teeth health, as well as muscle function.

🛡️

Antioxidants

Oranges contain plant compounds such as flavonoids that help protect cells from oxidative damage.
