Vitamins & Minerals in Banana

Bananas contain several vitamins and minerals that help the body perform important daily functions.

⚡

Vitamin B6

Helps the body process proteins and supports normal brain and nervous system function.

💚

Potassium

Supports normal muscle function, nerve signals and healthy blood pressure.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports the immune system and helps protect cells from oxidative damage.

🌿

🦴

🌱

🌱 UNDERSTAND YOUR NUTRITION

Banana & Nutrient Deficiency Awareness

Bananas contain nutrients that contribute to a balanced diet. Understanding what they provide can help you make informed food choices.

Vitamin B6

Bananas provide vitamin B6, which helps the body process proteins and supports normal nervous system function.

Potassium

Bananas provide potassium, an essential mineral needed for normal muscle function and fluid balance.

Folate

Bananas contain small amounts of folate, which supports normal cell growth and DNA production.

Important nutrition note

Eating bananas can contribute to your daily nutrient intake, but bananas alone cannot prevent or treat nutrient deficiencies. A varied diet and professional medical advice are important when a deficiency is suspected.
