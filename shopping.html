SMART FRUIT SELECTION

How to Choose Fresh Fruits.

Knowing what to look for can help you select fruits that are fresh, enjoyable and suitable for your needs.

👀

Check the Appearance

Look for fruits with their natural colour and avoid those with extensive bruising, mould or visible damage.

✋

Feel the Texture

Gently check the firmness of fruits. Different fruits have different textures when ripe.

👃

Check the Smell

Many ripe fruits have a pleasant, natural aroma. Avoid fruits with unpleasant or fermented smells.

⚖️

Compare Quality

Compare the condition and price of different fruits before deciding what to purchase.

KEEP YOUR FRUITS FRESH

Store Fruits the Right Way.

Proper storage can help maintain fruit quality and reduce unnecessary food waste.

❄️

Refrigerate When Needed

Store many ripe fruits in the refrigerator to slow down ripening and help maintain their quality.

🌡️

Keep Some Fruits at Room Temperature

Some unripe fruits, such as bananas, can be left at room temperature until they ripen.

🧺

Store Carefully

Avoid crushing delicate fruits. Remove damaged pieces and follow suitable storage practices.

SHOP WITH CONFIDENCE

Smart Tips for Every Shopping Trip.

A little planning can help you save money, reduce waste and make better food choices.

📝

Make a Shopping List

Write down the fruits you need before shopping to help you stay organised and avoid unnecessary purchases.

💰

Compare Prices

Compare prices from different sellers and consider the quality and quantity before making a purchase.

📅

Buy According to Your Needs

Purchase quantities you can reasonably consume before the fruits spoil.

🌱

Consider Local Options

Explore locally available fruits and seasonal options in your community.
