Pawpaw Nutrition Facts

Pawpaw is a naturally sweet tropical fruit containing dietary fibre, vitamin C, vitamin A and other nutrients.

Nutrition per 100g

Raw pawpaw
43 Calories (kcal)
10.8g Carbohydrates
1.7g Dietary Fibre
0.5g Protein
0.3g Total Fat
7.8g Natural Sugars

Values are approximate and may vary depending on pawpaw variety and ripeness.

🧡 ENJOY EVERY BITE

Delicious Ways to Enjoy Pawpaw

Pawpaw is naturally sweet, versatile and easy to include in everyday meals. Here are some delicious ways to enjoy it.

🧡

Eat It Fresh

Cut a ripe pawpaw in half, remove the seeds and enjoy the soft, sweet flesh with a spoon.

🥤

Pawpaw Smoothies

Blend ripe pawpaw with milk or yoghurt for a creamy and refreshing smoothie.

🥗

Fresh Fruit Salads

Combine pawpaw with fruits such as mango, pineapple and banana for a colourful salad.

🍋

With a Little Lime

Add a small squeeze of fresh lime juice to ripe pawpaw for a bright, refreshing taste.
