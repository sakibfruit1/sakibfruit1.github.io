🌿 VITAMINS & MINERALS

Essential Nutrients in Apples

Apples contain several vitamins, minerals and plant compounds that contribute to everyday health.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

⚡

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🦴

Vitamin K

Contributes to normal blood clotting and healthy bones.

🌿

Vitamin B6

Helps support normal energy metabolism and nervous system function.

🛡️

Polyphenols

Natural plant compounds with antioxidant properties, found especially in the skin.

✨

Antioxidants

Help protect cells from oxidative damage as part of a balanced diet.

🌿 UNDERSTAND YOUR NUTRIENTS

Apple Nutrient Awareness

Apples provide some important nutrients, but they should be enjoyed as part of a varied diet.

🍊

Vitamin C

Vitamin C contributes to normal immune function and collagen production. Apples provide a small amount.

⚡

Potassium

Potassium helps support normal muscle function, nerve signalling and fluid balance.

🌿

Dietary Fibre

Fibre supports healthy digestion and helps maintain regular bowel movements.

Important: Apples contribute to a healthy diet, but eating apples alone cannot prevent or treat nutrient deficiencies. Include a variety of nutritious foods in your daily meals.
