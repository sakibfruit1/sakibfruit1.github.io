🍉 KNOW WHAT YOU EAT

Watermelon Nutrition Facts

Watermelon is a refreshing fruit with high water content, natural sugars, vitamin C and other important nutrients.

Nutrition per 100g

Raw watermelon
30 Calories (kcal)
7.6g Carbohydrates
0.4g Dietary Fibre
0.6g Protein
0.2g Total Fat
6.2g Natural Sugars

Values are approximate and may vary depending on watermelon variety and ripeness.

🌿 EXPLORE THE NUTRIENTS

Vitamins, Minerals & Plant Compounds

Watermelon contains essential vitamins, minerals and natural plant compounds that contribute to a balanced diet.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

🥕

Vitamin A

Watermelon provides beta-carotene, which the body can convert into vitamin A.

⚡

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function and fluid balance.

🌱

Vitamin B6

Helps the body process proteins and supports normal brain function.

💧

Magnesium

Contributes to normal muscle and nerve function.

🍉

Lycopene

A natural plant compound with antioxidant properties that gives watermelon its red colour.

❤️ DISCOVER THE GOODNESS

Health Benefits of Watermelon

Watermelon provides hydration, essential nutrients and beneficial plant compounds that support different functions in the body.

💧

Supports Hydration

Watermelon contains about 92% water, making it a refreshing way to contribute to your daily fluid intake.

🛡️

Supports Immune Function

Its vitamin C content contributes to the normal functioning of the immune system.

🍉

Provides Antioxidants

Lycopene and vitamin C help protect cells from oxidative damage.

✨

Supports Skin Health

Vitamin C helps the body produce collagen, an important protein for healthy skin.

❤️

Supports Normal Heart Function

Potassium contributes to normal muscle function, including the heart muscle.

🌿

A Refreshing Light Snack

Watermelon is naturally low in calories and can be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet.

🍉 ENJOY EVERY BITE

Delicious Ways to Enjoy Watermelon

Watermelon is versatile and easy to enjoy. Here are some simple ways to include it in your everyday meals.

🍉

Eat It Fresh

Cut watermelon into slices or cubes and enjoy it as a naturally refreshing snack.

🥤

Fresh Watermelon Juice

Blend fresh watermelon for a refreshing drink. You can enjoy it without adding sugar.

🥗

Fruit Salads

Combine watermelon with fruits such as pineapple, mango and orange for a colourful salad.

❄️

Chilled Watermelon

Refrigerate watermelon before serving for a cool and refreshing treat.

🌿 UNDERSTAND YOUR NUTRITION

Nutrients Your Body Needs

Watermelon contains nutrients that contribute to normal body functions. Here are some nutrients it can help provide.

Vitamin C

Helps support normal immune function, collagen production and iron absorption.

Vitamin A

Supports normal vision, immune function and healthy skin.

Potassium

Helps support normal muscle function, nerve signalling and fluid balance.

Important Nutrition Note

Watermelon can contribute to your daily nutrient intake, but it cannot prevent or treat nutrient deficiencies on its own. A varied, balanced diet is important for good nutrition.
