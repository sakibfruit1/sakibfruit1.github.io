Pineapple Nutrition Facts

Pineapple is a refreshing tropical fruit that provides vitamin C, manganese, dietary fibre and natural sugars.

Nutrition per 100g

Raw pineapple
50 Calories (kcal)
13.1g Carbohydrates
1.4g Dietary Fibre
0.5g Protein
0.1g Total Fat
9.9g Natural Sugars

Values are approximate and may vary depending on pineapple variety and ripeness.

🍍 ESSENTIAL NUTRIENTS

Vitamins & Minerals in Pineapple

Pineapple contains several vitamins and minerals that contribute to important functions in the body.

🍊

Vitamin C

Supports normal immune function and helps the body produce collagen.

🌿

Manganese

Helps support normal bone development and energy metabolism.

⚡

Vitamin B1

Helps the body convert food into energy and supports normal nerve function.

🩸

Folate

Contributes to normal cell division and red blood cell formation.

💧

Potassium

Helps maintain normal muscle function and fluid balance.

✨

Antioxidants

Pineapple contains plant compounds that help protect cells from oxidative damage.

🍍 FRESH & DELICIOUS

Different Ways to Enjoy Pineapple

Pineapple is sweet, juicy and versatile. Here are some simple ways to enjoy it.

🍍

Eat It Fresh

Cut fresh pineapple into small pieces and enjoy it as a refreshing snack.

🥤

Fresh Pineapple Juice

Blend fresh pineapple into a refreshing drink. Keep the pulp for extra fibre.

🥗

Fruit Salads

Combine pineapple chunks with other fruits for a colourful fruit salad.

🥣

With Breakfast

Add pineapple pieces to oatmeal, yoghurt or your favourite breakfast.
