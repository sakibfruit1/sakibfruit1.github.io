Pineapple Nutrition Facts

Pineapple is a refreshing tropical fruit that provides vitamin C, manganese, dietary fibre and natural sugars.

Nutrition per 100g

Raw pineapple
50 Calories (kcal)
13.1g Carbohydrates
1.4g Dietary Fibre
0.5g Protein
0.1g Total Fat
9.9g Natural Sugars

Values are approximate and may vary depending on pineapple variety and ripeness.
